# A living world inside the body

**Direction revised 7 September 2026 after the crowded brain was rejected.** This supersedes the single flat whole-body level and the C01–C04 brain layouts. The accepted Heart Model A remains protected.

## The structural change

The body atlas and the playable world have different jobs. The atlas makes anatomy and connections understandable. A playable organ is an expansive location that is framed around Attention and the next landmark. Its entire outline does not need to fit on screen during play.

Each organ has its own level scale, local lighting, movement character and environmental sound. Doorways and light passages connect these locations. Detailed rooms load as locations; the atlas uses deliberately simplified organ silhouettes. This prevents the world's anatomical arrangement from dictating tiny playable rooms.

The body is alive. Routes flex gently, wind carries soft seeds, couriers queue at valves, and light travels through tissue. Care changes a physical situation in the location: a route opens, a pool clears, a flow becomes smooth, or a landmark grows. A numerical health meter is secondary.

## Brain: a folded garden landscape

The brain is a landscape bounded and divided by living cortex. The awareness tree is a landmark within that landscape. It does not occupy most of the floor or cover the whole playable view.

The revised first playable section has three legible places:

1. **Arrival meadow.** A generous quiet space, a visible small Attention character and a first bright thought. The tree is visible beyond a clear path. No prop collection or branching junction blocks the arrival.
2. **Awareness court.** A warm, open gathering space around a smaller sculpted tree. Its roots visibly accept bright thoughts. There is enough floor to approach, turn, set down a thought and see the result. The main path skirts the court and leads toward the water crossing.
3. **Quiet thought garden.** A raised garden across a broad stone bridge. The focus lotus and worry inhabitants share a clear open court. Returning three thoughts grows a root crossing back to the arrival side, completing a readable loop.

The design removes the competing library display, extra ornamental trees, isolated cerebellum prop, overlapping paved loops and scattered lanterns from this first section. Later brain districts can include memory terraces and a cerebellar movement garden, but each must earn its own space and purpose. They do not become decorative objects scattered across the first clearing.

The C05 model expands landscape coordinates by 1.55 in both horizontal axes—about 2.4 times the plan area—while reducing the main tree to 0.68 of its previous size and keeping collectible thoughts and the lotus at their previous object scale. These are design choices, not anatomical measurements. A source-scene Attention proxy makes scale visible. The resulting interior is approximately 65 by 67 game meters; the outer tissue footprint is larger. Exact navigation area is reported by the build audit.

## Connection rules

- Attention's light passages form a clear travel network. Air, blood, food and urine remain distinct environmental flows.
- First visits reveal the location by walking. A restored organ opens a short route for repeated daily care.
- Connections have visible departure and arrival landmarks, consistent light color and a recognizable doorway silhouette.
- The main care path does not require precision jumping, narrow balance beams or a hidden interaction prompt.
- A camera view should show one dominant landmark, the player, the route forward and at most one nearby side invitation.

## Art and review order

Review spatial composition at native player size before dressing. Then review anatomical landforms, the hero model, construction at joins, materials, vegetation and lighting. Adding more props cannot repair a weak layout.

Use Figma refs 358 and 384 for cortex and garden geography; 275 and 307 for the awareness tree, thought inhabitants and their currents; 354 for lung branching. These are owner-supplied concepts, not screenshots of the prototype. The approved heart render defines the protected heart baseline.

Every next location must pass the same checks: readable arrival, generous turning space, a complete route, meaningful care, a visible environmental change, a return path, controllable camera and unobstructed Attention. A compiling build or a connected navigation mask is not visual approval.

## Current implementation boundary

C01–C05 brain studies were rejected. C05's larger floor did not fix its inflated cortex, arbitrary joins, primitive surfaces or weak composition. Its completed package is preserved as rejected evidence. The next construction follows the generated Brain Location Key Art, grounded in Figma references 358, 275, 307 and 379, with independently built terraces, incised cortex topology, textured materials and a physical thought encounter. Most other organs still require redesigned models and implemented journeys. The original whole-body map is preserved as evidence; it is not the target quality or the definition of the final game's spaces.
