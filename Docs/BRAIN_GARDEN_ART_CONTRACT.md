## Brain level drawing

The tree occupies the central clearing. Its spreading canopy is a visible landmark from the entrance, but the broad foreground ring stays open for the character. Six subordinate areas surround it: memory meadow at the entrance, a small thought library, a planted idea grove, synapse pools, the focus terrace, and a worry garden overlooking a folded cerebellum. The cortex is sculpted as continuous folded tissue around this landscape rather than a smooth bowl divided into slots.

Use a main loop and two shorter cross connections. Broad paths curve around planted beds, cross the stream on two stone arches, and climb gently to rear terraces. The stream and tree bed are physical navigation boundaries. Small flowers stay at path margins. Individual landmarks use modeled bark, roots, leaves, masonry, water edges and planted ground, with restrained surface detail on open walking areas.

The middle tree receives the heartbeat carried from the heart. Seed memories gathered around the garden nourish it. Small thoughts can be released with SPACE; the focus terrace provides a hold interaction. Camera occlusion may fade decorative canopy and foreground cortex, while interactive trunks and stations remain visible. Native movement, floor heights, obstacles and bridge clearance must be checked against the visible geometry.
