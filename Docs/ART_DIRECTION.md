# A living body adventure

The owner's current direction overrides the dense early cutaway studies: cute, welcoming, anatomically recognizable, quiet enough to play. A stylized isometric adventure is the primary experience. The complete body is an atlas, available on Tab.

## Visual rules

- One primary organ landmark per local view. Organ silhouette first, interaction second, decoration last.
- Wide soft light bridges communicate movement. No hard gold-edged road network or pipes laid across the player route.
- Each organ gets one clear identity: breathing and wind in the lungs; pulse, chambers and flowing blood in the heart; drifting thoughts and one quiet awareness garden in the brain.
- Retain right/left placement, right lung's three lobes and left lung's two, four heart chambers, the brain's two hemispheres, and the major abdominal organs. Heights, sizes and open passages are deliberately adapted for play.
- Keep at least a character's width of space on each side of the main path where practical. The main bridge widths are 220–250 cm. The cute attention figure has an approximately 100 cm wide silhouette after its readability enlargement.
- Most decorative detail belongs at room edges. Interactive objects stay visible; scenery fades when it blocks the player.
- Warm peach tissue, cool breath, coral heartbeat, restrained lilac thought-space. No gore or clinical gloss.

## Research applied

These are design interpretations of inspected references, not claims that their layouts have been reproduced.

- [TUNIC](https://tunicgame.com/): a small character in a larger isometric adventure. Applied as closer character-scale composition, readable local spaces, and a useful body atlas instead of the default distant anatomy view.
- [The Last Campfire](https://thelastcampfiregame.com/): gentle adventure, a vulnerable luminous character, and hopeful discovery. Applied as warm material choices, the silent attention ranger, gentle movement, and one calm focal garden.
- [Runic's Hob level-design notes](https://www.runicgames.com/blog/2016/04/28/building-the-world/): linked spaces, looping approaches, interactive cues and the world responding to the player. Applied as routes around both lungs, the rising brain connection, local thought interactions, and visible organ response to care. Today's slice does not implement Hob-like world transformation or a full progression system.
- [Splashteam](https://en.splashteam-games.com/) and [BodyQuest](https://www.didactoons.com/bodyquest-the-human-body-adventure/): inspected for friendly scale and a body that can host an adventure. Their art assets are not used.
- [Pixar's Inside Out](https://www.pixar.com/inside-out): inspected for a readable emotional inner world. Applied as an awareness garden and distinct resource colors, without copying characters or settings.
