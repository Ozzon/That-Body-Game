## Spatial contract

Two hemispheres with a central fissure. The cortex occupies its own bounded footprint around connected garden terraces. Entry at front left; spring at rear left; thought nests at rear right; release pool at front right; awareness court at the center. A broad circuit visits all five. An initially dormant root crossing shortens the return trip after care. Paths at least 4.8 m wide, character capsule 0.68 m diameter. Dimensions are game design choices, not anatomical measurements.

## Encounter contract

Explore from entry, follow a thought current, meet the nest inhabitants. Carry a bright star to the tree using a physical grab. Drag a heavy cloud to the release pool. Swat a light wisp with a directional impulse. An amber knot requires repeated, readable pushes. Focus at the lotus calms the currents. The tree receives light through its roots, canopy warms, return crossing opens and the morning concludes at the entrance. Retry resets the encounter without reloading the art.

Character needs grounded locomotion, turn lean, anticipation, carry and dragging poses, swat follow-through, blink/gaze, foot contacts and secondary cloth motion. Physics serves tactile contact, weight and lag; world navigation remains reliable. Effects are tied to events and currents, not constant decoration.

## Art contract

Hand-shaped surface topology with incised sulci; no inflated capsule ring. Fitted masonry and continuous support beneath terraces and bridges. Directional bark, pointed leaves with veins, bounded plant beds and an airy tree crown. UV mapped tissue, stone, bark and cloth, with separate roughness/normal detail. Broad warm key, lavender shade, cool spring fill, amber tree bounce; restrained cel shade bands retain surface paint. Inspect complete location, actual player view and tree encounter before packaging. C05 remains rejected. Generated concepts do not establish visual acceptance of a model.
